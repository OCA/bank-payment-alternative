When a payment order pays (or collects from) another company of the
database, this module books the mirror entry in that company at upload:
one line per payment line on the account of the linked inter-company
invoice generated by ``account_invoice_inter_company``, reconciled with
it, and the net amount on the outstanding receipts (or payments) account
of its bank journal.

A payment line without linked invoice is booked on the partner's
receivable or payable account. It is left unreconciled, with a message
on the payment order, as is a line whose linked invoice is not posted.

The receiving company is the company whose partner is the payment
partner. Its bank journal is found from the bank account of the payment.
No company found: no mirror entry.

Single currency only: the mirror entry books the amount of the payment
line in the receiving company's currency, without conversion.

Configuration
=============

In the receiving company, the bank journal must be linked to the bank
account used in the payment order.

The outstanding account of the mirror entry is the outstanding receipts
(or payments) account of the first incoming (or outgoing) payment method
of that bank journal which has one; otherwise the outstanding receipts
(or payments) account of the chart of accounts.
