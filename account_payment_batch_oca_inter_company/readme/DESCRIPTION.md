When a payment order pays (or collects from) another company of the
database, this module books the mirror entry in that company at upload:
one line per payment line on the receivable (or payable) account,
reconciled with the inter-company invoice generated by
`account_invoice_inter_company`, and the net amount on the outstanding
receipts (or payments) account of its bank journal.

The receiving company is the company whose partner is the payment
partner. Its bank journal is found from the bank account of the payment.
No company found: no mirror entry.
