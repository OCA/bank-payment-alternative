This module adds netting to outbound payment orders: receivables of a
partner (customer invoices, supplier refunds, debit miscellaneous
entries on any reconcilable account) are added to a supplier payment
order as negative lines, so that only the net amount is transferred.

For each transfer group (partner, bank account, currency), the payment
entry has one liquidity line for the net amount and one counterpart line
per payment line, on the account of its journal item. On upload, each
counterpart line is reconciled with its journal item, partially if the
payment line amount is lower than the residual. Receivable and payable
balances stay gross.

Configuration
=============

On an outbound payment method line used for payment orders, check
**Netting**. Payment method lines without this option keep the standard
behaviour.

On a netting payment method line, payment lines are grouped without the
account and the date of their journal items, and all payment lines take
the date of the order: the scheduled date if the order uses a fixed
date, today otherwise.

Usage
=====

Add the receivables to net to a supplier payment order: the amount of
their payment line is negative, and can be lowered to net only part of
the residual.

On confirmation, the order is refused if the net amount of a transfer
group is negative, even when the partner's total over all its bank
accounts is positive.

Limits:

- A line with a structured reference cannot be netted: the bank file
  would carry the reference of a single invoice for a net amount.
- The module does not check the legal conditions of netting (insolvency
  proceedings, public creditors, consumers, ...): the accountant remains
  responsible for the receivables they net.
